# Phone basics (fold into the "Keep by the phone" block)

General call-handling habits from common customer-service and inside-sales practice. Edit
them to suit the business; the config's `KEEP_BY_THE_PHONE` and `KEEP_TIPS` are where they
show up in the kit.

## Use
- **Say who you are first.** Your name and the business in the first sentence, then why you
  are calling. A lead who is unsure who is on the line stops listening.
- **Check it's a good moment.** One quick question ("have I caught you at an okay time?")
  before you go into detail. If not, agree a specific time to call back.
- **Open questions, then summarise.** Ask "what" and "how" questions, take notes, and play
  back a one-line summary before you suggest anything, so they know you heard them.
- **Clarify a concern before you answer it.** Ask one short question to understand what
  is behind it, then answer that, not the concern you expected.
- **Plain words.** No jargon or internal terms. If a word needs explaining, swap it.
- **Choice of two times.** Offer two specific slots rather than an open "when suits you?".
- **Know the offer and its conditions.** Name, what is included, any deposit or condition,
  expiry. Read it from the written terms, not memory.
- **Confirm back and log it.** Repeat the day, time and what they will get, then note the
  call in the CRM before the next one.

## Leave out (unless the business asks for it)
Pressure tactics: forced silences, invented deadlines or "only one slot left" when it is not
true. They undermine trust and can breach healthcare advertising rules. If the business wants a
firmer close, use real deadlines only and keep the tone kind.
